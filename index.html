After the program

The program is where you start. Incubation is where we build together.

For businesses IBCN chooses to back, incubation is a deeper partnership. IBCN works alongside the founder to build the business, with hands-on support such as mentor guidance, documentation and procurement help.

In return, IBCN takes an equity stake in the business. Terms are agreed in writing before incubation begins, and the 3-month program is separate from it.

Planned founder platform

Knowledge matters. Execution changes things.

Every week follows the same rhythm, so progress never depends on motivation alone.

  1. Weekly execution tasks

    Each week you get clear tasks with due dates and priorities, so you always know what to do next.

  2. Business milestones

    Targets that show how far the business has come, and what is still ahead.

  3. Mentor feedback

    Written feedback on the work you submit, so every task teaches you something.

  4. Progress reviews

    Scheduled check-ins against your goals, to keep you honest and moving.

Questions founders ask.

Where details are not final, we say so instead of guessing.

Who can apply to IBCN?

Students, aspiring entrepreneurs and people with a business idea, as well as existing business owners who need support. Detailed eligibility criteria will be published before applications open.

Do I need an existing business?

No. You need a business idea or a business you want to build. Existing businesses that need support are also welcome.

Can I apply with only a business idea?

Yes. The program starts by understanding your business idea and refining it, so an idea is enough to begin.

What does the program include?

A 3-month program based on real-life business knowledge and case studies, not textbook theory. You refine your idea, plan your go-to-market, and work with mentors. The final curriculum is not yet approved.

What is the program duration?

3 months.

What is the program fee?

The program fee is shared with you at the time of enrolment. Documentation and legal support is a paid service, available separately. This website does not take payments.

How does founder mentorship work?

Mentors give guidance on your idea, your go-to-market plan and your launch. How mentors are assigned and how often sessions take place will be announced. Founders who go on to run their businesses are intended to become IBCN mentors.

Does IBCN take equity?

Equity applies to incubation, a separate stage for future businesses IBCN chooses to support after the program. Terms are agreed in writing before incubation begins.

Does IBCN guarantee funding?

No. IBCN does not guarantee funding, investment, revenue or business success. The results depend on the work you do.

How do applications and selection work?

Planned process: you submit an online application, it is reviewed, some applicants are shortlisted for a conversation, and each applicant is then told whether they are accepted, waitlisted or not selected. The online application is not open yet.

How much time will I need each week?

To be announced with the program details.

When do applications open?

To be announced.
